Our Beginnings

Rooted in Tanzania

House of Gems began with a simple purpose: to bring the best that Tanzania's gemstone and jewelry industry has to offer to the world. More than three decades later, that purpose still guides everything we do.

Today, as part of AG Group, we operate fifteen outlets across Tanzania, with our flagship House of Gems showroom in Arusha and The Tanzanite Dream in Dar es Salaam.

Corporate Social Responsibility

Giving back to Tanzania

Tanzania's gemstones belong to its people and its land. We believe the trade that grows from them should support the communities around it.

  • Responsible, vetted sourcing from Tanzanian suppliers
  • Supporting local communities and livelihoods
  • Encouraging sustainable practices across the supply chain
