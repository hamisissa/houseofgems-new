Tanzanite

Merelani Hills · Manyara

The blue-violet treasure of Tanzania, found nowhere else on Earth.

Tsavorite

Simanjiro · Manyara

A vivid green garnet with exceptional brilliance, rarer than emerald in fine sizes.

Mohs 7 – 7.5On the map

Ruby

Winza · Morogoro · Longido

Tanzania produces rich red rubies, as well as the striking ruby-in-zoisite of Longido.

Mohs 9On the map

Sapphire

Tunduru · Songea · Umba

Blue sapphires and a rainbow of "fancy" colours — pink, yellow, orange and purple.

Mohs 9On the map

Spinel

Mahenge · Morogoro

Mahenge is celebrated for neon pink and red spinel of remarkable intensity.

Mohs 8On the map

Rhodolite & Malaya Garnet

Umba Valley · Tanga

Raspberry-red rhodolite and the peach-to-orange Malaya garnet, first found in the Umba Valley.

Mohs 7 – 7.5On the map

Chrome Tourmaline

Landanai · Manyara

A deep green tourmaline coloured by chromium and vanadium.

Emerald

Lake Manyara

Emeralds are mined near Lake Manyara, often alongside alexandrite.

Mohs 7.5 – 8On the map

Alexandrite

Tunduru · Lake Manyara

The colour-change gem — green in daylight, red-purple under incandescent light.

Mohs 8.5On the map

Zircon

Natural zircon with fiery brilliance, in blues, golds and earthy browns.

Diamond

The Williamson mine at Mwadui is one of Africa's historic diamond mines.
