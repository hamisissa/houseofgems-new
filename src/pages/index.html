Exquisite Tanzanite

Found in only one place on Earth

Tanzanite is mined exclusively in the Merelani Hills, in the shadow of Mount Kilimanjaro. Its velvety blue-violet colour shifts with every turn of the light — making each stone as unique as the person who wears it.

As the home of Tanzanite, we offer a fine selection of this rare gemstone — loose stones hand-picked by our gemologists, and finished jewelry crafted by our own artisans.

Who We Are

Tanzania's Premier Gemstone & Jewelry Experts

For more than three decades, House of Gems has brought the very best of Tanzania's gemstone and jewelry industry to the world. From our flagship showroom in Arusha and a network of fifteen outlets, we ethically source, cut and craft every piece we sell.

Whether you are searching for a single loose Tanzanite or a one-of-a-kind piece of jewelry, our experts will guide you every step of the way.
